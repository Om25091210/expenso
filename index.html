The monthly method

  1. Add up all three accounts. That single total is all the money you have until the next income lands.
  2. List every bill before spending anything. Recharges, Wi-Fi, travel (trips × cost per trip). These are promised money, not free money.
  3. Give each account one job. One small account pays fixed bills, one pays daily spending, and the biggest stays as your reserve. Match bills to the account that covers them closest, so small leftovers don't sit idle.
  4. Set the food money as a daily limit. Divide it by the days left. Spending under the limit one day rolls over to the next.
  5. Pay bills the day they're due, from the assigned account only. Don't pay a bill from the reserve unless an account is short.
  6. Leave the reserve alone. What's left at month end is savings or a cushion for next month, not extra spending.
  7. Check once a week. Update balances here. If spending is ahead of the daily limit, cut the next few days to catch up.
